This baseline check was made in order to determine the cause of variability among starting pressures of the various sensors, which is visible in the data. Having built the sensors with the needles attached, the needles were uncapped and open to the air next to a bunsen burner for relative sterility. The needles were then sprayed with ethanol and used for the following experiment. It seems based on this data that the sensors show different numbers for the same pressure, indicating that they are calibrated differently and ought to be checked against more accurate and precise sensors.

Note that the sensor number only corresponds to the port on the dock and does not show a particular sensor independent of that.
